About FixIt

FixIt makes it simple for students to report campus problems, so they reach the right people and don’t get lost.

How it works

Reporting a campus problem is simple. Follow these four steps.

1

📍 Identify a problem

Spot something broken, unsafe or missing on campus.

2

📝 Submit a report

Add a category, description and location to explain the issue.

3

🔖 Keep your reference

Save your reference number so you can find your report later.

4

📊 Check the status

Open View Reports to follow your report’s progress.

Category guide

Choose the category that best describes the problem you have noticed.

🛠️ Facilities & Repairs

Broken furniture, doors, roofs, fittings and other building repairs.

⚡ Electricity

Faulty lights, power cuts, damaged sockets and wiring.

💧 Water & Plumbing

Leaks, water shortages, blocked drains and broken taps.

📶 Internet & IT

Wi‑Fi outages, lab computers and projector faults.

🧹 Cleanliness & Waste

Overflowing bins, dirty washrooms and littering.

🛡️ Safety & Security

Poor lighting, unsafe paths, broken locks and hazards.

♿ Accessibility

Blocked ramps, broken lifts and barriers to movement.

💬 Other

Any campus problem that doesn’t fit the categories above.

Frequently asked questions

Answers to common questions about reporting campus issues.

What types of problems can I report?

Broken facilities, electricity faults, water leaks, internet outages, cleanliness issues, safety concerns and accessibility barriers. If your problem doesn’t fit a category, choose Other.

Why should I provide an accurate location?

It helps staff find where the problem is, so it’s easier to investigate and fix.

How can I check the status of a report?

Open View Reports, search by your reference number or title, then open the report to see its progress.

What does each status mean?

Pending: the report has been received and is awaiting action.
In Progress: the problem is being handled.
Resolved: the problem has been addressed.

What should I do in an emergency?

FixIt is not an emergency-response service. Contact campus security or the relevant emergency service directly.

Spotted a problem?

It takes about two minutes to report. Help us make our campus a better place.
